Updated 2026-11-10 15:45 UTC · 248 battery-electric cars from 2022 on · 20.000 km/yr · capital at 7% · best selling year chosen per car (max 12 yr)

Market: EVs lose about **17% of their value per year** at this mileage (fitted on all 169 EV listings, older ones included). Costs in today's krónur. Assumptions: `config.yaml`.

<details><summary>How the number is built</summary>

cost per km = (price − resale after N years + capital cost + charging + kílómetragjald + insurance + service + tyres + tax/skoðun + expected battery repairs after warranty) ÷ km driven. Resale comes from today's market: what the same model sells for N years older and 20.000·N km further on. N is whichever year gives the lowest cost per km.

</details>
